FAQ

Questions, answered

How often should I use Dr. Pooper?

For most homes, one packet per month keeps the bacterial population strong and your system healthy. Larger households or older systems may benefit from more frequent dosing.

Is it safe for all septic systems and pipes?

Yes. Dr. Pooper is non-toxic, non-corrosive, and safe for all standard septic systems, pipes, and plumbing — as well as RV and marine holding tanks.

How do I use it?

Drop one water-soluble packet into your toilet and flush. The packet dissolves and releases the bacteria and enzymes directly into your system. That's it.

Will it get rid of septic odors?

Absolutely. By digesting the waste that causes smells, Dr. Pooper eliminates odors at the source rather than just covering them up.
