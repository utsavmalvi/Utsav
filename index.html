FAQ

Questions, answered

How is Dr. Pooper different from other septic treatments?

Most treatments just add a small packet of bacteria to your tank. Dr. Pooper's patent-pending Accelerator formula instead makes the naturally-occurring bacteria already in your system reproduce and grow 10–40× faster — so there are truly enough bacteria to eat the solids and clean your system.

What systems does it work in?

Aerobic, anaerobic, cesspools, sand mound systems, commercial septic systems, and even pet waste disposal systems. It's non-toxic and safe for all pipes and plumbing.

How do I use the tablets?

Drop a fast-dissolving tablet into your toilet and flush. One 20-tablet eco-pouch provides 10 months of complete system maintenance — follow the label for dosing.

Will it get rid of septic odors?

Yes — odors are typically eliminated within hours. By multiplying the bacteria that digest the waste causing smells, Dr. Pooper kills odors at the source rather than masking them.

What won't it clear?

Dr. Pooper clears organic clogs, scum, and sludge. It does not clear roots or inorganic objects like toys and other foreign items — those need mechanical removal.
